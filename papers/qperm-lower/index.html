Lower bounds for the quantum permanent

Suvrit Sra · TU Munich, Department of Mathematics, School of CIT

Version v1 · released 24 October 2026 · 30 pages

PDFSupplementGitHubTimestamp

Abstract

Griffiths's 1969 conjecture on the positivity of Schur forms reduces to the positivity of the quantum permanent $\Phi_n$ on positive bistochastic maps. This conjecture was refuted by Du with a map for which $\Phi_9<0$. Therefore, we ask: how negative can $\Phi_n$ be? We prove a lower bound $\Phi_n\ge-3n/(3n^2+20n+103)$ (for $n\ge 8$), and an asymptotically stronger bound $\Phi_n\ge-1/n+(3e^2-7-o(1))\log n/n^2$. The key to our bounds is the orbital polynomial of the map, which turns $\Phi_n$ into a linear function of a distribution on partitions and positivity into linear constraints. We also construct explicit maps for $n=8, 9$ that improve on Du's example. Finally, if $d_*\in\{4,\ldots,8\}$ is the first dimension for which $\Phi_n<0$, the minimum $\mu_n$ of $\Phi_n$ over positive bistochastic maps satisfies $-\mu_n=\Omega(n^{-d_*})$; we conjecture the matching order $-\mu_n=O(n^{-d_*})$.
